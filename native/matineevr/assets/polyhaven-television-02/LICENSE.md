The original glTF, geometry buffer, and 1K diffuse, normal, and ARM textures
are preserved here. `steam-frame/prepare-crt-assets.py` bakes placements into
`crt-models.bin`. `crt-materials.rgba` combines diffuse, ARM, and normal maps
in that order, with rows flipped for the native upload convention. Source
textures and geometry remain unchanged. Eight instances share one 1K set.

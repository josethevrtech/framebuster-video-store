# Professional lounge CRT

This work is based on **Sony PVM-1341 || Sony Playstation** by **dark_igorek**:
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sony-pvm-1341-sony-playstation-5f80311f1a4646ef9673f20ee6dc6245

Public archive mirror:

The artist identifies the PVM-1341 as a late-1980s/early-1990s professional
13-inch monitor. This adaptation retains only its PVM meshes and enlarges
the asset to a 1.42-metre overall width for the commercial-sized lounge display.
It is an oversized period-style recreation, not a dimensionally exact PVM-1341
or a claim to reproduce a different Sony model. Included PlayStation and
controller meshes are excluded; the app keeps its separate console assets.
UVs are repacked, diffuse/roughness/metallic/normal maps baked at 1024 pixels,
glass darkened for a powered-off screen, and local ambient occlusion baked
offline. The source creator's texture work is retained in derived maps.

Generated runtime files: ../lounge-tv-model.bin and ../lounge-tv-materials.rgba.
Build scripts: steam-frame/prepare-lounge-tv.py, bake-fixture-occlusion.py,
and pack-lounge-tv.py. Runtime rendering needs no network asset downloads.
